Questions

Cost, names and how the scores work.

What does Lockr cost?

Nothing. Lockr is free and runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install.

Who can see my name in the app?

Anonymous mode is on by default. Your posts go out under a handle like anon gargoyle, and friends see a count where you lock in.

How is a spot scored?

You rank spots head to head. A ranking of n spots scores them from 10 at the top down to 5 at the bottom.

How long do I stay locked in at a spot?

Until you end the check-in. One you forget stops counting after 8 hours.

How long can a post on Lockr be?

240 characters at most. Each post goes to friends only or to the whole campus.

Which study spots are in it?

18 spots across 14 buildings, from Mansueto Grand Reading Room to Plein Air Café.
